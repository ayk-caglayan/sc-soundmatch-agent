# Perceptual-Neural-Physical Sound Matching (Han et al., ICASSP 2023)

**Authors:** Han Han, Vincent Lostanlen, Mathieu Lagrange
**Venue:** ICASSP 2023
**PDF:** SynthMatch-HanHan-PNP-ICASSP-2301.02886v2.txt

## Summary

Conference paper introducing **PNP loss** -- the optimal quadratic approximation of spectral loss that runs as fast as parameter loss. Uses physical modeling synthesis (drums) as decoder and JTFS as spectral representation. Demonstrated on matching synthetic drum sounds. Conference predecessor to the full [TASLP 2024 paper](han-pnp-taslp-2024.md).
