# Synthesizer Sound Matching Using Audio Spectrogram Transformers (Bruford et al., DAFx 2024)

**Authors:** Fred Bruford, Frederik Blang, Shahan Nercessian
**Venue:** DAFx 2024 (Late Breaking Results)
**Affiliation:** Native Instruments
**PDF:** SynthMatch-AST-Bruford-DAFx-2024.txt

## Summary

Applies **Audio Spectrogram Transformers (AST)** as the encoder architecture for synthesizer sound matching. Motivated by the need for general-purpose matching across diverse commercial synthesizers. Industry perspective from Native Instruments.
