# Quality-Diversity for Synthesizer Sound Matching (Masuda & Saito, JIP 2023)

**Authors:** Naotake Masuda, Daisuke Saito
**Venue:** Journal of Information Processing, Vol. 31, 2023
**PDF:** MasudaAndSaito2023-QualityDiversity-SoundMatching-31_220.txt

## Summary

Introduces a **quality-diversity** approach to sound matching using novelty objectives added to genetic algorithm search. Discovers diverse collections of matches spread across interpretable spectral features, rather than a single best match. Useful for creative exploration where synthesized imitations with unique character are valued.

## Connections

- GA-based approach contrasts with neural methods in [InverSynth](inversynth-2018.md)
- Diversity objective relates to latent space exploration in [Flow Synthesizer](flowsynth-2020.md)
